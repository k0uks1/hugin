A query ?- atom. prints the values of its variables for which the atom is a fact.

A body may also negate an atom (not road _ X) and aggregate over the solutions of a formula (count, sum, min, max). Both read a relation that must be complete when they are evaluated, so the compiler orders the relations into strata: road is complete before start is computed, and start and reach before fanout. A program in which a relation depends on itself through a negation or an aggregate is rejected. The meaning of such a program is its perfect model: the least model of each stratum in turn, with negations and aggregates read in the strata below.

_ is a variable that occurs once. In not road _ X it is local to the negation: start X holds if X has an outgoing road and no road enters it.

Part 2 has a lesson about proofs over a relation defined with not; aggregates have the same treatment in the library (std/strata), and are not used further in this tour.
