A Hugin program has two levels. The object level is Datalog with types: it declares relations, states facts and derives new facts with rules. The meta level, introduced later in this tour, runs at compile time; it can build object programs and, the subject of this tour, prove things about them.

city : type. declares an object type, and berlin, paris, rome, oslo : city. its constants. road : city -> city -> rel. declares a relation with two columns of type city. A fact is an atom followed by a period.

A rule head :- body. derives the head for every assignment of its variables (uppercase names) for which every atom of the body is a fact. The two rules of reach are the usual transitive closure: a road is a way, and a road followed by a way is a way.

The program's meaning is its least model: the smallest set of facts that contains the given facts and is closed under the rules. %output reach. prints the facts of reach in it. Arguments are type-checked: reach berlin 3 is a compile error.

Try: add road rome oslo. and see the output grow to every pair of cities.
